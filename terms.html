The meet rules

These apply to chat, names, crew names, plates, liveries, car files, photos and anything else you share in the game.

  • No hate or harassment: nothing that attacks people for who they are, and no bullying, threats or stalking.
  • No sexual content, and nothing that sexualizes anyone under 18.
  • No violence, self-harm, drugs or weapons content.
  • No personal details: don't share your own or anyone else's address, phone number, school or real-life plans to meet.
  • No spam, scams, ads or links to sell things.
  • No pretending to be someone else, including us.
  • Photos must be yours: only your own car or coffee. Other people's plates and faces stay blurred; showing your own plate is your choice.

Typed words are checked before anyone sees them, and some things can't be sent at all. Who can type, pick a name or add photos depends on age, as the game explains when you sign in.

What happens when someone breaks them

Depending on what happened, we remove the content, mute the player for a day or longer, take away their typing, reset their scores, or close their account. Serious cases, like anything that puts a child at risk, are reported to the authorities. If you think we got it wrong, write to us.

Fair play

  • No cheating: no changing the game, its saves or its traffic, no bots, and no using bugs to win.
  • One account per person. Don't use extra accounts to win leagues, cups or gifts.
  • Times on the boards are checked. We remove ones that can't be real, and the accounts behind them.
