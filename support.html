Support

titef.beckham@gmail.com

Tell us your name in the game and what happened. A screenshot helps. Or write from the game itself: Account, then Send feedback, and our answer shows up there.

Report someone

Tap their name, their car or their message, then Report. Block is right there too: someone you block can't see you, message you, race you or send you anything, and you won't see them. Your block list stays with your account, and you can unblock people under Account, then Blocked players. Reports are read every day, and clear cases are hidden straight away.

If someone is in danger, call your local emergency number first. Then report them in the game, and email us with "Safety" in the subject (or send feedback in the game that starts with "Safety").

Delete your account

On the map, tap Account (top right), then Delete my account. Then confirm.

Your account, car, photos, friends, scores, feed cards and messages are removed from our server straight away, and Sign in with Apple is disconnected from the game. The game on your phone keeps its own progress until you delete the app. You can also ask us by email.

Chat and ages

The game reads your age range from Apple's age sheet. If you skipped it, tap Account on the map, then Share your age range. Reading your car or coffee from a photo needs a shared age range of 13 or over, and Apple's age sheet needs iOS 26 or later.

EveryoneQuick phrases, emotes and reactions. Players under 13 get a name picked by the game.
13+Pick your own username.
16+Type in chat, and write your car's story. Every typed word is checked before anyone sees it.
18+Add real photos of your car and an Instagram handle to your profile.

Purchases

Bought the Garage Pass or the Supporter Pack and can't see it? Open Account in the game and tap Restore Purchases, on the same Apple account you bought it with.

Both are looks and garage space only. Neither one makes a car faster. For refunds, Apple handles payments: use reportaproblem.apple.com.

New phone

Sign in with the same Apple account and everything comes back: your cars with their parts and saved setups, your beans, career stars and bests, your looks, car file, profile, league and crew. If the new phone already has progress of its own, the game asks which to keep.

The meet is quiet

The meet shows whoever is playing right now. When it's quiet, race someone's best run, play Today's Challenge, or come back at the top of the hour for rush hour, when rare cars pass more often.

Something isn't working

The game runs on any iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Email us with your phone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when it happened. Or send feedback from the game: it brings your phone model and iOS version with it.
